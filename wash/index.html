Dispatch No. 01 · The Wash

The first thing you touch every day.

Before the coffee, before the inbox, before the road. A few notes on The Wash, the hand wash we're known for, and why the sink deserves better than an afterthought.

From Ghost Town Ranch · A Frontier Modern apothecary

Wide light, small rituals

We restored a mid-century modern home on three acres in the California high desert. The light is wide, the air is dry and the wind gets into everything. Hands take most of it. Fence wire, steering wheels, gas pumps, dog leads, the door of every roadside diner between here and wherever you're headed.

You wash them twenty times a day and never once think about it. That was the problem.

Most hand soap is an afterthought. A pump of something blue, scented like a fruit that never existed, left by the sink because it came with the house. It strips your hands, it smells like a rental car, and nobody has ever asked what it is.

We wanted the opposite. Something you notice on the first pump. Something a guest picks up and turns around to read the label. Something that makes the most ordinary minute of the day feel like it belongs to you.

The sink is the first room of the day. It should smell like it.

The grapefruit

Cold-Pressed Grapefruit is the one people ask about. It's sharp and bright with zero sweetness. Not the candy version. More like the peel splitting under your thumb early in the morning, when the house is still cold.

It cuts through on the first lather and leaves the room smelling clean in a way that reads expensive without trying. We've been told it's the best grapefruit people have ever smelled. We'll let you be the judge.

It's our top seller, and the scent we'd hand you first.

What's actually in the bottle

The point of a good hand wash is that it cleans without leaving your hands tight and squeaky. So the base is aloe juice instead of water, with jojoba and avocado oil, shea butter and hyaluronic acid to keep hands soft after the rinse. Betaine is the gentle, plant-derived cleanser that does the work.

It carries the ECOCERT COSMOS NATURAL certification, which means an independent body audits what goes into the formula and the packaging it comes in. We think that standard is worth explaining, because it's the reason we can stand behind every ingredient on the list.

  • Key ingredientsAloe juice, jojoba oil, betaine, shea butter, berry extract, flower extract, avocado oil, hyaluronic acid, Alteromonas ferment extract
  • CertifiedECOCERT COSMOS NATURAL
  • Leaves outDyes, parabens, phthalates and synthetic fragrance. Vegan and cruelty-free.
  • Size16.57 fl oz, in a recyclable PET bottle

Oversized, on purpose

The bottle is big. That's deliberate. A generous size means fewer reorders, and a black band with the white Bull means it looks right left out on a concrete counter, a vintage basin or the shelf of a guest bath. No decanting into something prettier. It's already the prettier thing.
