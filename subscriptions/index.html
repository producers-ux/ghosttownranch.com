Questions.

How do I manage my subscription?

Go to your account and sign in with the email you ordered with. We send a one-time code, no password needed. From there you can skip, reschedule, change the delivery frequency, update payment or cancel.

What can I subscribe to?

Hair, bath + body and skincare: the things you use up. Makeup, candles and gift sets are one-time only.

Can I skip or cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your account. There is no minimum number of orders and no fee to cancel.

When am I charged?

Your first order is charged at checkout. Each refill is charged on the day it is scheduled to ship, and we email you before it does.

Can I subscribe to more than one product?

Yes. Add each one with Subscribe selected. Check out together on the same schedule and they ship together.

Does the 10% first-order code work too?

Your first order can use STAYNOTORIOUS. After that, refills ship at the 20% subscription price.

Something not right?

Email howdy@ghosttownranch.com and we will sort it out.
