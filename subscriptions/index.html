Questions.

What is a Standing Order?

A regular delivery you set once. You choose the products and how often, every 6, 8 or 12 weeks, and we send them on that schedule at 20% off until you say stop. You can skip, change or cancel anytime.

How do I manage my Standing Order?

Go to your account and sign in with the email you ordered with. We send a one-time code, no password needed. From there you can skip, reschedule, change the delivery frequency, update payment or cancel.

What can I put on a Standing Order?

Hair, bath + body and skincare: the things you use up. Makeup, candles and gift sets are one-time only.

Can I skip or cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your account. There is no minimum number of orders and no fee to cancel.

When am I charged?

Your first order is charged at checkout. Each delivery is charged on the day it is scheduled to ship, and we email you before it does.

Can I put more than one product on a Standing Order?

Yes. Add each one with Standing Order selected. Check out together on the same schedule and they ship together.

Does the 10% first-order code work too?

Your first order can use STAYNOTORIOUS. After that, each delivery ships at the 20% Standing Order price.
