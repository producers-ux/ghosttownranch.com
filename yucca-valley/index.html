Ghost Town Ranch, Yucca Valley

The flagship for Frontier Modernism, in a brand spanking new space.

There is no dust. This is Frontier Modernism.

The idea

Picture a farmstand that went to design school. White walls, black steel, and a canopy of olive branches and dried flowers hanging from the ceiling near the entrance. We skip beige. The full Ghost Town Ranch collection, plus a handful of guest brands and artists we pick by hand.

It is not traditional retail. It is a room for creators, artists, and brands with a point of view. Show your work and sell it your way.

We are sharing this with a select few. Before we build, we want to know who is in. That is what this is for.

We have a hunch that people want something different.

The ethos

Frontier Modernism is the ethos we developed for Ghost Town Ranch: raw frontier, modernist polish.

Three collections, made for every body. Wash House. Routines. Ritual.
